# Figure quality gate

- Preserve data, units, uncertainty, and sign conventions.
- Use vector SVG for plots and diagrams when the target accepts it.
- Use colorblind-safe colors plus line style or marker redundancy.
- Keep text legible at final publication size; avoid shrinking a desktop-sized plot.
- Label axes with quantity and unit; define normalization explicitly.
- Avoid truncated axes unless justified and disclosed.
- Keep multi-panel labels consistent and ordered by the manuscript narrative.
- Export raster panels at the requested physical size and at least 300 DPI for print.
- Record input paths, column mappings, transformations, generation command, and date.
