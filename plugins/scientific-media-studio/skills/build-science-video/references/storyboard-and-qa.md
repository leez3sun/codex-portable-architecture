# Storyboard and video QA

For each beat record: time range, narration, on-screen text, visual source, camera/motion, audio cue, and scientific invariant.

Use this narrative skeleton only as a starting point:

1. Hook with the engineering consequence or counterintuitive result.
2. Establish model, scale, and boundary conditions.
3. Reveal the mechanism through synchronized simulation and diagrams.
4. Compare cases quantitatively.
5. State limitations and uncertainty.
6. End with the decision or research takeaway.

Reject uniform slideshow pacing, repeated transitions, unlabeled solver footage, and decorative motion that competes with evidence.
